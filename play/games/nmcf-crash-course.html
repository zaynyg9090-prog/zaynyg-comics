Choose your beast — Chomp starts free, the rest unlock by scoring touchdowns.

Dodge the Redline Raiders (black & red). Grab green energy orbs to fill the BEAST METER. When it is full, smash the BEAST MODE button and run through everybody.

MADDEN-STYLE DRIVE: 4 downs to gain 10 yards — watch the yellow FIRST DOWN line on the field. Gain 10 for a fresh set of downs; get stopped on 4th down and it is a turnover (the drive restarts from that spot). Touchdown every 100 yards, then kick the PAT: tap KICK PAT (or spacebar) when the needle is in the green for +1. Watch your STAMINA bar — sprinting and dodging drain it, easing off refills it, and running on empty slows you down. 3 hits and you fumble it away — but every 2 touchdowns and every golden LIFE ORB earns +1 life (max 5 helmets).
