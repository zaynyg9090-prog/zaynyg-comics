Zaynyg World: Comics & Games – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Zaynyg World: Comics & Games (the “App”, Android package com.zaynygworld.app, and its web version) is made by Zaynyg World, Jose Quinones, Fernandina Beach, Florida, USA. The App is designed for families, including children. We care about kids' privacy and collect as little as possible.

What we collect

We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, photos, contacts, location, or any other personal information. The App has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat, no social features, and no analytics of its own.

Saved progress

Game saves, reading progress, and the pages you unlocked are stored only on your own device. They never leave your device, and uninstalling the App deletes them.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The optional Zaynyg Pass subscription and single-comic unlocks are sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment under the Google Privacy Policy; we never see or store card details. The App only receives from Google Play which products this Google account owns, so it can unlock them. Every purchase screen in the App is behind a grown-ups check. You can cancel the Pass anytime in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version of the Android App shows a small banner ad and optional “watch a short video” ads that unlock a comic page or let you keep playing. Ads are served by Google AdMob. Every ad request is marked as directed to children and under the age of consent, is limited to content rated G, and asks for non-personalized ads only. The App does not use the Android advertising ID (the permission is removed). To deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may process limited technical information such as the IP address and device and app information, as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Any purchase removes ads. The web version shows no ads.

Links

The Shop tab opens the Zaynyg World store (zsbzba-dy.myshopify.com) in your web browser, only after a grown-ups check. That website has its own privacy policy.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. We aim to comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the Google Play Families Policy, and we use only Google Play Families self-certified ad technology. If you believe a child's information was collected, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices

You can use the App without buying anything. Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data. You can contact us any time with questions or requests.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and post the new version at this address.
